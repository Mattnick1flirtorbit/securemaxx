Document and Data Security, Built Into the Platform

The documents your organisation runs on — contracts, claims, employee files, patient records — carry obligations as well as information. On Outcome Cloud™, a document is controlled from the moment it arrives, opened only by the people whose role allows it, and every action taken on it becomes part of its record.

Where documents are exposed

Most organisations don't have an information problem — they have a location problem. Contracts live in email inboxes. Invoices sit in a shared drive. A signed form is on one person's desktop and a scan of it is on someone else's. When information is scattered like that, nobody can say who has seen a document, which version is current, or where it has been.

That scatter is the exposure. Access can't be enforced in a dozen places at once, and copies multiply every time a file is forwarded. Security starts by ending the scatter: one repository, one security model, one record of what happened.

How a document is protected across its life

Outcome Cloud™ protects a document at every stage of its life, not just in storage. Intake is controlled: documents arrive into a governed queue — by email, upload, scan, or form — instead of landing in personal inboxes. Access is permission-based: role-based access is enforced everywhere, so people see only what their role allows.

Every view and action becomes part of the record's history. The Outcome Repository™ keeps a complete audit history on every document and record: what arrived, what process touched it, who opened it, who approved what, and when. Retention is governed by policy rather than by hope: retention, classification, and access rules apply consistently, in the AWS region you choose.

Protection, stage by stage

Stage 1

Controlled Intake

Arrival is a system event: documents enter one governed queue under one security model — no uncontrolled copies in inboxes.

Stage 2

Classification

Each document is identified and indexed on arrival, so the access and retention rules for its type apply from the start.

Stage 3

Validation

Data is checked against your rules before anyone acts on it, and every check — passed or flagged — joins the record's history.

Stage 4

Review & Resolution

Exceptions reach only the people authorised to decide them, and each decision is recorded with a name and a timestamp in the document's history.

Stage 5

Repository

The finished record is retained in the Outcome Repository™ under your retention policy — searchable, governed, and audit-ready.

Answering the auditor's three questions

Every serious review comes down to three questions: where did it go, who could see it, and can you prove it? A scattered file landscape can't answer any of them cleanly. A governed platform answers all three from the same record: the journey is its audit history, the access list is the permissions actually enforced on it, and the proof is the trail itself — kept under your retention policy.

That is also the thinking behind Private AI on MaxxLogix: policy-based controls decide who can run what, on which data, and every AI touch on a document is logged — what ran, on what data, when, and under whose authority. Your data is never used to train someone else's model. Security is a property of the architecture — not a promise in a brochure.

Frequently asked questions

Who can see our documents on the platform?

Only the people whose role allows it. Role-based access is enforced everywhere on Outcome Cloud™ — repository, workflows, and search — so access follows the permissions you set, not whoever a file happened to be forwarded to. And because access is part of the record, you can show who could see a document at each stage.

Can we prove what happened to a document?

Yes — that is what the audit history is for. Every document and record in the Outcome Repository™ carries a complete history: when it arrived, what processes and people touched it, what was decided, and when. When an auditor asks, the answer comes from that trail, not from memory.

Where does our data live?

Outcome Cloud™ runs on AWS cloud infrastructure, and records are kept in the AWS region you choose — the region your regulations require. For AI workloads, Private AI offers deployment tiers — virtual private cloud, private, on-premises — so sensitive work can run inside your own control boundary.

How are healthcare documents handled?

Healthcare organisations use the platform for HIPAA-compliant document management: intake, claims, referrals, and records handled with audit trails and access controls, a BAA available, and hosting on AWS under a signed AWS BAA. The same repository, permissions, and history apply to patient documents.

Is "secure" here just a marketing claim?

A fair question, worth answering plainly. MaxxLogix publishes a formal Security & Trust Center (v1.0, July 2026) describing layered protection across cloud infrastructure, identity management, information management, AI, and platform operations — ask for it and read what it says. But notice what this page has not done: shown you a badge as proof. Security you can rely on is architectural — a defined data boundary, permissions enforced everywhere, an audit trail you can inspect. Those are the claims made here, because they are the ones you can verify.
